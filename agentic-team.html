The Agentic Team

How a coordinated group of specialized AI agents plans, builds, and verifies work together.

What is an agentic team?

An agentic team is a small group of AI agents, each with a narrow role, that collaborate under a shared orchestrator to complete a larger task. Rather than relying on a single model to do everything, work is decomposed and routed to the agent best suited for each step — mirroring how a human team splits planning, research, implementation, and review.

This division of labor improves reliability and speed: each agent operates with a focused context and toolset, the orchestrator keeps the overall goal in view, and results are checked before they're considered done.

  • Orchestrator — breaks the goal into tasks and assigns them.
  • Researcher — gathers context, reads code, and answers open questions.
  • Coder — implements the changes.
  • Reviewer — checks the output for correctness and quality.
  • Tester — verifies behavior before it ships.
